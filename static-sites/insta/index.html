Insta Viewer

Open your Instagram export to read old chats by date and browse your posts, stories and reels. Everything stays on your device.

No need to unzip. If Instagram split it into several zips, pick them all together. You can also drop them here.

How to get your export
  1. In Instagram, open Settings, then Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions, then Download your information.
  2. Pick your Instagram account and "All available information". Choose the format JSON.
  3. When Instagram says it's ready, download the zip file(s) and open them here.
